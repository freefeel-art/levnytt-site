ROLE: STRATEGIC

# LevNytt — Objectives

## Primary Business Objective (Owner-set)

Build and operate a profitable Swedish NeoLife organic acquisition asset:
convert organic search and AI discovery into NeoLife customer and distributor
conversions, producing NeoLife revenue through Sponsor-ID 41-830928.

## Operating hierarchy (Owner-set, binding)

NeoLife revenue/conversions
→ NeoLife customer/distributor conversion opportunities
→ qualified organic traffic
→ search/AI visibility
→ content and authority production.

This ordering is the objective hierarchy. Traffic, rankings, indexed pages, AI
citations and content volume are **leading indicators** — they are not the final
business outcome and must not be optimized at the expense of NeoLife results.

## North star

**NeoLife business results** — NeoLife customer/distributor conversions and
attributable revenue. All other metrics are diagnostic.

## How progress is measured

1. **Direct NeoLife revenue/conversions** — the final outcome. Not yet
   technically available: there is no NeoLife back-office/affiliate reporting
   access. This is the identified missing attribution path (Owner boundary —
   requires NeoLife back-office credentials/API).
2. **NeoLife conversion opportunities** — first-party click events on
   Sponsor-ID links (customer shop + distributor registration) via the
   Cloudflare D1 `levnytt-cta-events` store. This is the strongest truthful
   measurable proxy and the attribution path to implement next.
3. **Qualified organic traffic** — Google Search Console clicks/impressions/
   position. Available and authoritative today.

Do not fabricate conversions or revenue. When direct measurement is unavailable,
report the proxy and the missing path, never an estimate.

## Guardrails (binding)

1. **Evidence-first identity preserved.** Fakta före hype; no fabricated
   claims, no income promises, no results guarantees.
2. **Preserve Sponsor-ID monetization.** All shop/registration links keep
   Sponsor-ID 41-830928.
3. **NeoLife only.** OLSP does not belong to LevNytt. No OLSP objectives,
   attribution, conversion targets, runtime state, content strategy, or
   capabilities govern this project.
4. **Cashbackkollen isolated.** Cashbackkollen objectives/attribution/runtime
   never govern LevNytt.
5. **Language.** Public content Swedish (sv-SE); code, commits, documents,
   report structures English; Owner communication Finnish.

## Explicitly not objectives

- Traffic, impressions, rankings, indexed pages, or article counts for their
  own sake (diagnostic leading indicators only).
- OLSP signups, OLSP sales, or any OLSP outcome.
- Publishing cadence or content volume quotas (outputs, not outcomes).

## Current measured baseline

- NeoLife revenue: **unmeasured** (no back-office access).
- NeoLife link-click conversions: **not yet instrumented** (D1 store exists,
  client beacon pending).
- Organic traffic: **~2 clicks / 366 impressions per rolling 28 days** (GSC,
  2026-06-30..2026-07-28), avg position ~31.5, CTR ~0.55%.
