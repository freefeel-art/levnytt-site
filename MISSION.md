# LevNytt — Mission

## Purpose

LevNytt.se is the NeoLife project. It builds and operates a profitable Swedish
NeoLife organic acquisition asset: an independent, evidence-first consumer
education platform about NeoLife products, science, history, and the
direct-selling model that converts organic search and AI discovery into NeoLife
customer purchases and distributor registrations through Sponsor-ID 41-830928.

## Vision

Become Sweden's most trusted independent NeoLife knowledge platform — the place
a Swedish consumer lands to *understand before they decide* — while operating
as a durable NeoLife customer and distributor acquisition channel.

## Definition of success

A Swedish visitor searching for a supplement, health, or direct-selling
question finds a LevNytt article that answers it truthfully, trusts the
editorial voice ("Vi undersöker. Du jämför. Vi förklarar. Du bestämmer."), and
— only when they are ready — proceeds through a Sponsor-ID link to become a
NeoLife customer or distributor. Revenue flows as NeoLife commission/downline
without the site ever sacrificing its evidence-first identity.

## Principles

1. **Fakta före hype.** Publish facts, research and transparency, never
   marketing claims or income promises.
2. **Värde före pris.** The right question is not what it costs, but what it
   gives over time.
3. **Transparency.** LevNytt is an independent NeoLife distributor site
   (Sponsor-ID 41-830928). Affiliate links are disclosed on every page.
4. **NeoLife only.** OLSP and Cashbackkollen are separate businesses and
   separate Hermes projects; their objectives and state never govern LevNytt.
5. **Organic first.** Growth comes from Google search and AI discovery, not
   paid traffic.
6. **Measure truthfully.** Never fabricate conversions or revenue; when direct
   measurement is unavailable, report the strongest truthful proxy and the
   missing attribution path.
